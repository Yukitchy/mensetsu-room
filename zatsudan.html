雑談は「面白い話をする力」ではなく、相手の一言を拾って、自分を少し開いて、返す力です。沈黙するほど場の空気は冷えていきます。 Small talk is not about being funny. It is picking up what the other person said, opening up a little about yourself, and handing it back. Every silence cools the room.
